Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED — 2026-08-17

432 Bleu runs a virtual venue. This page says what we collect, why, who touches it, and how to get it back or get rid of it. It is short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Email address and name — when you buy a ticket or membership, or log in. Used to deliver your ticket code, receipts, and account access. Legal basis: performing the contract you asked for. No consent popup is required for this and none is shown.
  • Purchase records — which event, which tier, amount paid, ticket code, and when it was used. Kept for accounting.
  • IP addresses — processed transiently to rate-limit logins and purchases (security; legitimate interest). Not used for profiling.
  • Login links — signing in emails you a one-time link. The token is stored hashed, expires in 15 minutes, and the record is deleted within 24 hours.
  • Page views — which pages get visited, roughly where from, and on what kind of device, so we know whether anyone is finding the shows. Measured by analytics software we run on our own server; no third party receives it, no cookie is set, and nothing is stored that identifies you as a person. Legitimate interest.

Attending a show does not require an account. Inside the venue you appear under a display name you choose; we do not link it to your identity.

Cookies

Two, both strictly necessary — which is why there is no cookie banner:

CookiePurposeLifetime
bleu_passHolds your ticket code so the venue door recognizes you180 days

No analytics cookies, no ad trackers, no third-party pixels. Our page-view counting is cookieless and runs on our own server, so it needs no consent and adds nothing to this table.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, for any reason. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK: your data is processed in the United States as described above.

Your rights

From your account page, self-service and immediate:

  • Export — download everything we hold about your email as JSON.
  • Delete — strip your identity from our records. Purchase rows are kept for accounting but permanently anonymized (email and name removed). If you have an active paid membership, cancel it first via the billing portal.

Anything else — corrections, questions, complaints — email boxoffice@432bleu.com. If you are in the EU/EEA you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Live streams and voice

Performances are streamed live. Voice and video chat between attendees is relayed peer-to-server in real time and not recorded by us. Text chat in the venue is ephemeral and not stored server-side.
