# 432 Bleu

**A virtual concert venue.**

Live music, played live, in a world you can actually walk around in. Come as an avatar,
find a spot near the stage, and run into people the way you would at a real show.

We're still building. **Stay tuned.**

---

## Built on WorkAdventure

432 Bleu runs on [WorkAdventure](https://workadventu.re/) — an open-source platform for
building collaborative virtual worlds, made by [TheCodingMachine](https://thecodingmachine.com/).

It's a genuinely excellent piece of software. The proximity video and audio — where conversations
fade in as you walk toward someone and fade out as you leave — is the thing that makes a virtual
venue feel like a venue instead of a webinar. That single feature does more for the atmosphere of a
show than anything we could have written ourselves, and WorkAdventure gives it away for free, in the
open. Huge thanks to the team and the community around it.

If you want to build something like this yourself, start here:

- [WorkAdventure website](https://workadventu.re/)
- [Map building documentation](https://docs.workadventu.re/map-building/)
- [awesome-workadventure](https://github.com/workadventure/awesome-workadventure)

The upstream project README, including full development and self-hosting instructions, is preserved
in **[README-WORKADVENTURE.md](README-WORKADVENTURE.md)**.
