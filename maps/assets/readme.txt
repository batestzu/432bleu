

This compatibility kit includes some of the possible files you might receive in our tilesets.  It's a small sample of one of our tilesets (Urban Modern Exterior) with a little distortion on top.  Try loading these into your game engine and see what happens!  We've included information below on all the file types.


----------------------


All our tilesets come in TWO FORMATS:  

One of them is RPG Maker ready.  The other (for all other engines) is an "expanded" format that gives most possible arrangements of tiles, in both 48px and 32px tile sizes.  These are based on RPG Maker autotiles, but you do NOT need RPG Maker to use them.

Some engines (Unity, Godot, etc) have plugins that supposedly will allow you to use RPG Maker formatted tiles on your game maps.  We didn't test these, and we don't offer any support for them, but they just might work great for you!


----------------------


----------------------
GENERAL INFO
----------------------


A1:-------------------------------------------------------------
A1 is RPG Maker's animated ground tile.  (Usually water/waterfalls and so on in our tilesets.)

Each animation has three frames.  Feel free to experiment with this to see if you can get it to work in your engine.  

Due to many differences in game engines, we cannot provide separate animations for this.



A2:-------------------------------------------------------------
RPG Maker's standard ground tile format.  We always provide expanded versions of these. (See Ground and Walls)




Ground and Walls (same as a3/a4):-------------------------------------------------------------
This is the format that will work with most engines.  It's the one you will want to try first and the one we've tested with game engines for compatibility.  You'll get a group of these with every tileset purchase.  Their height and width may vary depending on how many tiles were in the tileset.



B-E:-------------------------------------------------------------
These files contain all the random decorative objects.  They should work with many engines as-is. 



Object Animation test format: -------------------------------------------------------------

As mentioned above, animations are hard to make work correctly with many game engines.  We've provided this format so you know what to expect when we say animations are included with a tileset.  If this setup will work for you, most of our animations probably will.

These animations often have a vertical rather than horizontal format, so plan accordingly.




Feel free to rearrange the tiles if they don't suit your workflow or engine preference.  We tried to make the tiles as easy to use as possible, but we understand that everyone works in different ways - you have permission to edit, just please don't post your edits (or any KR materials) on any website.  We do not plan to create additional tileset variations to support engines that require specific formats at this time.

We're not experts at any of these engines, so if you run into issues you're way better off asking Google or looking up Youtube tutorials.
